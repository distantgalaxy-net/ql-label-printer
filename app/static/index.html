Label Printer

Prints the smallest label your printer allows (about 13mm on most models).

Welcome! Set up your printer to get started. Tap Test and save: it checks it can reach the printer, then unlocks the label tabs.

Printer

Where to send labels. The printer needs a fixed IP address (set a DHCP reservation in your router) and must accept raw printing on the port below. Only the 62mm continuous roll (DK-22205) is supported.

Brother printers use port 9100. If printing is refused, turn Editor Lite off on the printer.
